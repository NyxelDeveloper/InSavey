## ✨ Key Features

InSavey is designed to be the most powerful and easy-to-use tool for saving Instagram media.

### 1. Save Everything in High Quality
*   **Photos & Videos:** Download any post in its original uploaded resolution.
*   **Reels Downloader:** Save your favorite Reels with audio in one tap.
*   **Stories & Highlights:** Download stories before they disappear (24-hour limit) and archive Highlights forever.
*   **Original Quality:** No compression. What you see is exactly what you get.

### 2. Private Content Access
*   **Private Saver:** Log in securely to download content from private accounts that you follow.
*   **Secure Session:** Your login is handled via official Instagram cookies on your device.

### 3. Smart User Experience
*   **Pop-up Share Feature:** The fastest way to download. Just click "Share" on Instagram and select InSavey—you don't even have to leave the Instagram app!

*   **Caption Copy:** Instantly copy post captions to your clipboard.
