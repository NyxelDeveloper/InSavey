## 2. Login and Account Sessions

The Application allows users to log in to their Instagram accounts via an in-app WebView browser.

- **No Password Storage:** We do not store your Instagram passwords. All login sessions are handled
  securely through official WebView cookies stored locally on your device.
- **Cookie-Based Sessions:** Session cookies are saved on your device to keep you logged in. You can
  remove them at any time by logging out or clearing app data.
- **No Server Upload:** Your login credentials and session data never leave your device and are not
  transmitted to our servers.
- **Multiple Accounts:** The Application supports managing multiple Instagram accounts. Each
  account's session is stored locally and independently on your device.

## 3. Media and Files Access

The Application requires access to your device's storage to save downloaded media.

- **Storage Permission:** On Android 11 and above, the Application requires "All Files Access"
  permission to save and manage downloaded content. On older Android versions, standard storage
  permission is used.
- **Local Processing:** All file operations (downloading, viewing, sharing) are performed locally on
  your device.
- **No Uploading:** We do not upload your photos or videos to any external server. Downloaded
  content is only saved to your chosen local directory.
- **User Control:** You can revoke storage permissions at any time through your device settings.

## 6. Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique
identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your
device's internal memory. This service does not use these "cookies" explicitly. However, the app may
use third-party code and libraries that use "cookies" to collect information and improve their
services. Additionally, the in-app WebView uses cookies solely to maintain your Instagram login
session, which is stored locally on your device.
